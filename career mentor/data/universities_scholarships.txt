A note on rankings: Most rankings below are drawn from the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2026 (the most widely used global subject ranking, released March 2026) and India's NIRF Rankings 2025 (Government of India, released September 2025). Rankings shift year to year — treat the numbers as a snapshot, not a permanent hierarchy, and always check the current-year ranking before applying.
